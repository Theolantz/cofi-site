Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

In short: CoFi collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Data you enter

The names, incomes and expenses you enter are stored only in the app's storage, on your device. The developer has no access to them.

This data is included in your iPhone backups (iCloud or computer) if you have enabled them. Those backups are handled by Apple according to your settings, not by CoFi.

No connection, no tracking

CoFi does not connect to the internet. The app contains no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party services. No account is required.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone erases all of its data.

Children

Since CoFi collects no data, no information is collected from children.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, for example by adding sync between devices, this page will be updated before that version is released.
